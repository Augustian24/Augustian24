### Hi, I'm August, founder of [innoCrafted](https://innocrafted.com)

innoCrafted is a technology company from India. We capture, organise and connect knowledge the internet is missing, and turn it into products people learn from and build with.

**What we're building**

| Product | What it is | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [iBeWonder](https://innocrafted.com/products/ibewonder) | iOS & Android learning app | Final testing, launching soon |
| [KnowledgeRepository](https://innocrafted.com/products/knowledgerepository) | API for structured knowledge about objects and learning concepts | In development |
| [WideLensStocks](https://innocrafted.com/products/widelensstocks) | Asian stock media site and datasets | Private preview |

**Labs**: software for makers: [Draftmorrow](https://innocrafted.com/products/draftmorrow), [innSwitcher](https://innocrafted.com/products/innswitcher), [Reposect](https://innocrafted.com/products/reposect) and [InnoDesign](https://innocrafted.com/products/innodesign). Our plan is to make Labs tools free for verified students, with paid licences for companies.

Most of our work is private while it is being built. Public projects will appear here as they open.
